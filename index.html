Before you go

Check the season

Haors and swamp forests are fullest after the rains. Hills and islands are easier in winter.

Carry cash

Remote boat operators and guesthouses often don’t take cards or mobile payment.

Hire local

Use local boatmen and guides. They know the water, and the money stays in the village.

Leave it clean

Carry your waste out. Many of these places have no bins at all.
